Skyward was a remix Daniel made for the Zelda tribute album 25YEARLEGEND.

00000008 was the background music for the TIGSource game K2. There was no official name given to the song so it goes by the default name given to it from the XWB bank. This has been converted to .mp3 as the original .wav extracted from the game doesn't work on most audio players.

Challenge Accepted. is an audio rip from a YouTube video Daniel submitted to a competition to become the composer for a game called Gunpoint. It contains brief previews of multiple songs with gameplay and sound effects overlayed. https://web.archive.org/web/20111205051422/https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hCUzFIPNVw0

Bigfoot is considered the rarest/hardest to find C418 track. It was created for a game jam called Action 51 Owns.

Best Song was created because someone in Discord told Daniel it was impossible for him to make a bad song, so he challenged that person by creating Best Song. Best Song 2 is the same thing pitched up so insanley high it breaks the audio. These are surpisingly still available on his SoundCloud, albiet privated.

marshmallows is a section of Höre from Bushes and Marshmallows (hence the file name), specifically the part at 6:46. This was shared via an Ableton project file on Twitter in 2015.

minecraft and Boo are previews of early versions and scrapped Minecraft songs. These were shared on Daniel's old blog.

Zelda Mindcrack was made during the Mindcrack 2017 livestream marathon as a challenge to create a "epic zelda remix". The full version was posted to Twitter shortly after.

All The Time was created for the Harmonix game Amplitude. An edited version of this appeared in 148 as Ample Time.

fire was posted to TIGSource on April 11, 2008 for a fire-themed music competition. This songs appears as Some really obscure ambient tune from Seven Years of Server Data in a slightly shortened form. It was previously lost but was recovered on July 17, 2021. 

march was created for a jam game at BIGJam 2009 made by qubodup. It sounds like a stock loop of marching drums but there are multiple sources confirming C418 did make it.

kihl2b was a collab between sohnemann and C418, posted on sohnemann's blog in 2008. There was an earlier version posted to C418's blog simply titled kihl, but that has been lost to time.

Flip vs sohnemann - October 11 is an early version of Phleps without any of C418's additions. It was shared by sohnemann on Discord in May 2022.

- drear - is an animation created by Camp Cult, a friend of C418 who used to do live visuals for him. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fTd8LwS8QqU

Intro Ambience and Promise were remixes of Silent Hill 2 tracks made for a fan demake, Soundless Mountain II. The music credits for it are very fuzzy but it's most likely that these two were Daniel's contributions.

kinda_cheesy_chiptune_song was the background music to a BIGJam 2009 game called Eat The Croissant, commonly refered to as simply Croissant. https://chrisjangames.wordpress.com/2010/08/28/eat-the-croissant/

(If there's anything wrong about these backstories lemme know.)
